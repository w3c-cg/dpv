dpv:RightsFulfilment has skos:broader pointing at a legal basis, not a purpose (w3c-cg/dpv#486)

Stratis: Do we have an use case or example of what we mean by Rights Fulfilment here?

Harsh: dpv:RightsFulfilment should have as parent dpv:FulfilmentOfObligation because rights may not be legal obligations

Stratis: When the legal basis is legal obligation but not a right,

Harsh: We also have an example on this in the purpose HTML, we should revisit describing this there as well -- https://dev.dpvcg.org/2.4-dev/dpv/modules/purposes#desc-legal-obligation

Georg: (and Harsh) We have Rights and Obligations fulfilment, but this does not cover cases where there are no bearer of rights/obligations. To clarify, we are only referring to purposes where rights/obligaitons have a bearer and there is some action or condition that needs fulfilment, which we have considered as an obligation in the hierarchy where fulfilment of obligation is the parent and rights fulfilment is a specific type of such fulfilment

ACTION: Change parent of dpv:RightsFulfilment to dpv:FulfilmentOfObligation

ACTION: Update purpose module description and examples for RightsFulfilment

Beatriz: (paraphrasing) we should think of expanding properties or another way to use purposes as legal basis or see what purposes can act as legal basis

Harsh: We have an intentional separation between purposes and legal basis because they represent different concepts. Purpose is describing what you are doing with data or AI, and the legal basis is describing where this requirement or authority is based in law. If we mix them, then people will start doing things such as legal basis is identity verification (for KYC) instead of stating it as purpose and using legal basis to state which exact law they are referring to.

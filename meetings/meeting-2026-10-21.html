EHDS extension updates

Beatriz: Beatriz, Julian, Christian, Gabriela and Marlin met on Monday, October 19th. The discussion was focused on the entities’ taxonomy. Currently, data holder concepts are only available in the DGA, health, and EHDS extensions (same applies for data users). We are proposing the addition of dpv:DataHolder to anchor these terms and future ones, e.g., Data Act extension should also extend this concept with its own definition of data holder.

Topic: Data Act 

Sheyla: We created an ontology from the Data Act

\ documentation: https://w3id.org/def/daont
Sheyla: we modelled articles 4, 8, 19
Beatriz: why these articles?
Sheyla: they correspond to the B2B, B2C, and B2G articles and we had this as a (small) scope
MuhammadAslam: Are there any updates as this work was also published earlier as a separate paper/preprint?

MuhammadAslam: I had a conversation with Meem before. The paper mentions that the ontology is useful for policy modelling. But in the paper, I did not find the policy. Am I right that you use SPARQL on the ontology to get answers. So how does this help with the policy modelling?
